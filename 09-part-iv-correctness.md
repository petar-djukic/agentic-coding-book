# Part IV — Correctness

*Outline stage — drafting notes are in `brainstorm/`.*

What "correct" means when the programmer did not write the code. Mechanical verification, inspection at a scale where reading everything is impossible, and confidence built from layers of evidence rather than from review.

The verification stack introduced in Chapter 1 gets its full treatment here: what each layer catches, what it misses, and what the residual risk is after all of them pass.
